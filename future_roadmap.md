# Reuni Platform — Architecture & Future Considerations

This document captures the architectural backlog and product considerations designed for the Reuni circular economy platform. While active development is parked and the codebase is preserved as a portfolio showcase, these notes document the technical roadmap and scaling strategies evaluated during development.

---

## Technical & Product Considerations

### Gamification & Engagement Enhancements

Conceptual enhancements to deepen user engagement and inter-campus friendly competition:

- **Achievement & Badge System:** A `badges` JSON column on `User`, populated during weekly/seasonal snapshot archival. Display earned badges on user profiles and trigger in-app celebration states. Badge tiers considered: weekly podium finishes (1st, 2nd, 3rd), seasonal champion, consecutive-week reuse streaks.
- **Cross-University Leaderboard:** Aggregating `kg_saved` by `university_domain` to produce a university-vs-university sustainability ranking (GDPR-safe, anonymised aggregated metrics). Toggleable via `FEATURE_MULTI_UNIVERSITY`.
- **Display Name & Moderation:** Allowing students to configure an optional custom alias for leaderboard appearances (distinct from institutional account names), backed by automated content moderation checks.

### ESG Report Exports (CSV/PDF)

Tooling for university sustainability offices and estate management teams:

- **CSV Data Export:** Granular data export of waste diversion statistics, kilograms saved, and CO₂e avoided categorised according to DEFRA/WRAP conversion standards.
- **Branded Executive Summary PDF:** Visual board-ready reports highlighting quarterly landfill diversion metrics for campus sustainability benchmarking.

### GDPR Data Portability (Subject Access Request)

Automated self-service compliance with UK GDPR Article 20:

- **Self-Service Export:** An on-demand JSON/ZIP archive in User Settings compiling account profile attributes, listing lifecycle histories, transaction timestamps, and anonymised chat logs.

### Event-Driven Notification Engine

Automating transaction loops and physical campus rendezvous:

- **Chat Activity Digests:** Transactional email alerts notifying students when an unread message arrives in an active claim thread.
- **Handshake Milestone Updates:** Instant confirmation notifications dispatched to both parties upon PIN verification and carbon metric registration.

## Scaling Architecture — Multi-Campus Role Hierarchy (Partner Lead)

### Context & Design Problem

The initial single-campus model (`admin`, `partner`, `student`) required platform admins to personally invite every sustainability team member. To scale across multiple UK higher education institutions without central administrative bottlenecks, a distributed management model was designed.

### Designed Hierarchy

```
Reuni Admin (Platform Owner)
├── Onboards universities (creates UniversityConfig records)
├── Promotes one partner → Partner Lead per university
├── Emergency account actions (deactivate a Partner Lead)
├── Platform-wide feature flags & maintenance controls
└── Cross-university aggregated observability

Partner Lead (Head of Campus Sustainability)
├── Invites additional partners scoped strictly to their own university domain
├── Configures academic term dates (Season schedules) for their campus
├── Accesses campus-specific ESG analytics and leaderboard statistics
└── Query-level tenancy isolation prevents cross-institution data leakage

Partner (Sustainability Team Member)
└── Reviews partner dashboard, monitors listings, generates impact reports

Student
└── Trades items, completes handshakes, earns carbon metrics
```

### Implementation Strategy

- **Attribute-Based Delegation:** Rather than creating a fourth role string across existing enum checks, an `is_university_manager = db.Column(db.Boolean, default=False)` flag on `User` designates Partner Leads.
- **Scoped Token Generation:** Invite tokens generated by Partner Leads embed their verified `university_domain`, ensuring self-registration cannot cross institutional boundaries.
- **Strict Data Isolation:** Database queries enforce tenancy isolation at the ORM layer matching `current_user.university_domain`.
